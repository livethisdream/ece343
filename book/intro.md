    <h3>Foundations</h3>
    <p>Fields and phasors, then the transmission line: propagation, reflection, matching, the Smith chart, and transients, measured on a network analyzer.</p>

::::{frame} Course Objectives
By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Solve transmission-line problems, including impedance matching, circuit parameters, and the Smith chart.
2. Explain the meaning of Maxwell's equations, in static and dynamic situations, and apply them.
3. Set up and solve electromagnetic propagation problems.
