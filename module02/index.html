    Module 2 — Electrostatics#

    ECE 343 · Module 02

    Electrostatics

    Charges at rest, and the vector calculus that describes their fields.

    Lessons 17–26 · Intro to Electromagnetics · Maj Ellis · Dr. Neil Rogers

    Where This Module Goes

    The vector calculus the rest of the course runs on, then the electric field of charges at rest: Coulomb’s law, Gauss’ law, potential, boundary value problems, boundary conditions, and capacitance.

    Chapter 4: Vector Analysis

    1. I can recognize and apply common vector calculus operations, such as: gradient, divergence, curl, and Laplacian in the three major coordinate systems.
    2. I can calculate surface and volume integrals in rectangular, cylindrical, and spherical coordinate systems.
    3. I can differentiate between Stoke's Theorem and the Divergence Theorem and apply them to various problems to simplify integrals.

    Chapter 5: Electrostatics

    1. I can apply Coulomb's Law to use direct integration to calculate the electric field due to charge density in various geometries, including rectangular, cylindrical, and spherical coordinate systems.
    2. I can apply the integral and differential forms of Gauss' Law to calculate the electric field due to charge density in various geometries, including rectangular, cylindrical, and spherical coordinate systems.
    3. I can calculate the electric field from the scalar electric potential in a variety of geometries.
    4. I can apply the electric field boundary conditions to calculate electric fields on both sides of a material boundary.
