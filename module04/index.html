    Module 4 — Dynamic Fields#

    ECE 343 · Module 04

    Dynamic Fields

    Let the fields change in time, and they leave as a wave.

    Lessons 33–41 · Intro to Electromagnetics · Maj Ellis · Dr. Neil Rogers

    Where This Module Goes

    Faraday’s law, displacement current, and the full set of Maxwell’s equations, then the uniform plane wave they predict: its properties, its polarization, and what happens when it meets a boundary.

    Chapter 8: Dynamic Fields

    1. I can articulate the significance of the displacement current and calculate it, given a time-varying electric field.
    2. I can articulate the meaning of and calculate the relaxation time for a variety of materials.
    3. I know the forms of Maxwell's equations for time-varying fields and use them to calculate electric and magnetic fields for a variety of physical systems.

    Chapter 9: Plane Waves (9.1-9.4)

    1. I understand the process of decoupling and finding solutions for Maxwell's equations for dynamic fields.
    2. I can articulate what a Uniform Plane Wave (UPW) is and describe key properties that relate the electric and magnetic field.
    3. I can calculate the electric and magnetic fields for a UPW.
    4. I can articulate the concept of polarization and determine the polarization for various UPWs.

    Chapter 9: Plane Waves (9.5-9.5)

    1. I can determine the reflection and transmission of UPW at a boundary for Transverse Electric (TE) and Transverse Magnetic (TM) waves, using boundary conditions.
