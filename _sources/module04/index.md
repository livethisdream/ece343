# Module 4 — Dynamic Fields

::::{frame}
:class: title-frame

<div class="course-mark">ECE 343 · Module 04</div>

<h1 class="frame-title">Dynamic Fields</h1>

<div class="title-rule"></div>

Let the fields change in time, and they leave as a wave.

Lessons 33–41 · Intro to Electromagnetics · Maj Ellis · Dr. Neil Rogers
::::

::::{frame} Where This Module Goes
Faraday's law, displacement current, and the full set of Maxwell's equations, then the uniform plane wave they predict: its properties, its polarization, and what happens when it meets a boundary.
::::

::::{frame} Chapter 8: Dynamic Fields

<ol class="lo-list" style="--module: '8'">
  <li>I can articulate the significance of the displacement current and calculate it, given a time-varying electric field.</li>
  <li>I can articulate the meaning of and calculate the relaxation time for a variety of materials.</li>
  <li>I know the forms of Maxwell's equations for time-varying fields and use them to calculate electric and magnetic fields for a variety of physical systems.</li>
</ol>
::::

::::{frame} Chapter 9: Plane Waves (9.1-9.4)

<ol class="lo-list" style="--module: '9'">
  <li>I understand the process of decoupling and finding solutions for Maxwell's equations for dynamic fields.</li>
  <li>I can articulate what a Uniform Plane Wave (UPW) is and describe key properties that relate the electric and magnetic field.</li>
  <li>I can calculate the electric and magnetic fields for a UPW.</li>
  <li>I can articulate the concept of polarization and determine the polarization for various UPWs.</li>
</ol>
::::

::::{frame} Chapter 9: Plane Waves (9.5-9.5)

<ol class="lo-list" style="--module: '9'; counter-reset: lo 4">
  <li>I can determine the reflection and transmission of UPW at a boundary for Transverse Electric (TE) and Transverse Magnetic (TM) waves, using boundary conditions.</li>
</ol>
