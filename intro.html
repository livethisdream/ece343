    Intro to Electromagnetics

    Where circuits stop being enough.

    Four modules, 41 lessons, from the transmission line to the plane wave.

    01

    Lessons 1–16

    Course Objectives

    By the end of this course you should be able to:

    1. Solve transmission-line problems, including impedance matching, circuit parameters, and the Smith chart.

    2. Explain the meaning of Maxwell’s equations, in static and dynamic situations, and apply them.

    3. Set up and solve electromagnetic propagation problems.

    How This Course Is Graded

    Every learning objective is scored on a binary 1 / 0 scale, with no partial credit. A 0 comes with detailed feedback rather than the answer, and if you submitted real effort on time you may revise your work until you earn the 1.
