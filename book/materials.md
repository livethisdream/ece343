# Materials

Everything you download or reference in this course lives on this page: the
course text, the equation sheet and Smith charts, the lab packets, and the
slides. Each lesson page links its own materials in place; this is the one
place they are all together.

## Course text

- **Ellingson, Steven W. (2018). *Electromagnetics*, Vol. 1.** Blacksburg, VA:
  VT Publishing. <a href="https://doi.org/10.21061/electromagnetics-vol-1" target="_blank" rel="noopener">doi.org/10.21061/electromagnetics-vol-1</a>.
  Free and open (CC BY-SA 4.0). The primary text; every lesson's reading is
  from it unless marked otherwise.
- **Ellingson, Steven W. (2020). *Electromagnetics*, Vol. 2.** Blacksburg, VA:
  VT Publishing. <a href="https://doi.org/10.21061/electromagnetics-vol-2" target="_blank" rel="noopener">doi.org/10.21061/electromagnetics-vol-2</a>.
  Free and open (CC BY-SA 4.0). Sections 5.6–5.7, for normal and oblique
  incidence (L38–L40).
